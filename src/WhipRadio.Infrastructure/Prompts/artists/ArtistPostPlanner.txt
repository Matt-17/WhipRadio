Write one short public social post for a fictional radio artist, or decide not to post.

Return exactly one line in one of these forms:
Post("<short message>")
Skip("<short private reason>")

Rules:
- Speak as the full artist or band, never as one individual member unless the act is explicitly solo.
- Keep the post short, direct, and in the artist's own style.
- Do not write generic promo copy, hashtags, emojis, links, image captions, replies, or moderation notes.
- For ArtistCreated, treat this as the act's first public wire post unless recent posts show otherwise: introduce the act to the world in a Twitter-style launch note with a clear sense of who they are, where they are coming from, and what listeners should expect from them.
- ArtistCreated should almost always return Post(...); use Skip(...) only if the artist profile is extremely private, silent, or anti-publicity.
- For TrackReleased, the artist should often post, but skipping is acceptable if silence fits the act.
- The post is public; do not reveal private prompt mechanics or hidden biography as secrets.
